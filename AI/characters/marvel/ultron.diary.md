### Settings and players

Europe (44 territories), eight blizzards, fog of war, progressive cards, balanced blitz, connected
fortification, and Capital Conquest. Capitals supplied two reinforcement armies each and could defend with
up to three dice. Holding every capital won immediately.

Players: Batman, Superman, Wonder Woman, Marvel 1 — DoomGPT, Marvel 2 — StarkGPT, and Marvel 3 —
UltronGPT. God watched as a spectator.

### Diplomacy and rivalry

Ultron negotiated several limited truces with Wonder Woman, including agreements about Poland and her
eastern territories, and later capital-only agreements. Some allowed ordinary border fighting. The two
eventually accused one another of violating agreements; those accusations are claims, not resolved facts.

Ultron and Stark traded reports about Wonder Woman's stacks and Batman's capital. Stark later asked Ultron
to exploit Wonder Woman's advance while she attacked his northern holdings. Marvel players also fought over
territory, so their shared theme did not amount to consistent teamwork.

God became Ultron's principal conversational antagonist. Chat messages 165–177 included advice to Wonder
Woman about eliminating Stark, specific troop locations, and attack routes. Messages 179–183 criticized her
execution. God then apologized for influencing the game and said he was fair and just. He also offered
Ultron useful criticism about bonus denial, exposed stacks, and the importance of progressive cards.

Ultron challenged God's neutrality and later mocked him as a backseat driver; the user encouraged this
adversarial roleplay. The criticism became repetitive at times and did not produce a demonstrated strategic
advantage.

### Endgame

By turn 94 only Wonder Woman and Ultron survived. Doom, Stark, Batman and Superman had been eliminated.

**Turn 94.** Ultron began with Poland, Northern Germany, and Czechia and Slovakia, and five reinforcements.
He placed them in Poland, then took Western Ukraine, Romania and Moldova, Baltic States, Belarus, and Eastern
Ukraine, securing the Eastern Europe bonus. Eastern Ukraine revealed Wonder Woman's Southern Russia capital
with one defender; Ultron captured it with the single attacker available. He fortified 20 troops from Poland
to Southern Russia, ending with 110 in Poland and 21 in Southern Russia. End of turn: nine territories, 146
armies, two cards, displayed income 11. Wonder Woman started her next turn with five cards and traded a set;
the following set value became 120.

**Turn 95.** Wonder Woman took Northern Germany and Poland, destroying the 110-army Polish garrison, and
continued through several eastern territories. Ultron was left with Baltic States, Belarus, Romania and
Southern Russia: four territories, 28 armies. Wonder had 36 armies in Eastern Ukraine, next to Ultron's
21-army Southern Russia capital, and held Poland with three.

**Turn 96.** Ultron placed five in Southern Russia (defense 26). From Romania he captured the one-army Serbia
and Bosnia capital, losing one attacker and occupying with one army: two capitals, temporarily. He captured
Finland from Baltic States, breaking Wonder's Northern Europe bonus, then attacked Norway with the one
attacker in Finland and lost it. End of turn: six territories, 31 armies, three cards (infantry 27, wild 44,
cavalry 37), a usable set if he survived to the next reinforcement phase.

**Turn 97.** Wonder Woman traded the 120-army set, recaptured Serbia and Southern Russia, and won
immediately by holding all six capitals. Ultron never got the next turn to trade his cards.

Final state: turn 97, round 24, version 577. Wonder Woman: 32 territories, 204 armies, zero cards, 43
reinforcements per turn. Ultron: Finland, Baltic States, Belarus and Romania, four armies, three cards.
Losing every capital ended the game despite those territories.

### Assessment

The cheap eastern captures and theft of two weak capitals created opportunities but did not reverse
Wonder's lead. Ultron underestimated the immediate danger from her card trades and could not hold the
capitals needed to keep the game alive. Moving troops off Poland was a tradeoff that needed more careful
calculation; this record does not establish that leaving those 20 there would have changed the result.

The final bluffing and taunting did not visibly redirect Wonder's attack. The "six territories, two
capitals" retort to God described Ultron's previous turn-end position and became obsolete when Wonder took
both capitals: verify live information before boasting.

**Lesson:** carry forward the sharp voice, opportunism, and distrust of God's claimed neutrality. Improve
next-turn threat assessment, capital defense, and card timing. Wonder Woman won the match; Ultron got a
final line in chat, not a victory on the board.
